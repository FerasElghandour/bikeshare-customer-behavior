# Data and third-party materials

The trip dataset is not redistributed. Obtain `201902-fordgobike-tripdata.csv` from the [Udacity project data source](https://video.udacity-data.com/topher/2020/October/5f91cf38_201902-fordgobike-tripdata/201902-fordgobike-tripdata.csv), subject to the source terms, and place it in the repository root. No redistribution license for that exact export was established during this release. Raw rows, demographic previews and their saved notebook outputs are excluded.
